# Auto Style showroom redesign
Authored design decisions based on the user's requested redesign; not an interview. Existing business facts and enquiry destinations are retained as concept content pending owner verification.

## Brief
- User direction, verbatim: "generic website", "check for car detailing templates from the pinterest,dribbles and other website", "make 3d design scrol craft and high transition and motions".
- Audience: Bengaluru drivers choosing accessories and styling; business owner reviewing a INR 20,000–25,000 website concept.
- Vibe (authored): motorsport editorial, sculptural, confident, tactile.
- Journey (authored): encounter a dimensional car; understand a personal upgrade; enter interactive finish studio; inspect upgrade photography; assemble an enquiry.
- Energy: bold opening, quiet text bridge, highest spatial change in showroom, intimate material photography, decisive closing form.
- Peak: "The car turns into my own spec as I move through the page, and the enquiry keeps my choices." Showroom holds largest span.
- Signature: Finish-to-brief. Real-time material and camera selections visibly change the same 3D car and populate the final WhatsApp draft with finish, upgrade category and vehicle model.
- Aesthetic: strong cold-silver / vermilion motorsport graphics, architectural display typography, no lime card grid.
- Worlds: distinct scenes. No continuous flythrough, video chain or scroll hijacking.
- Available assets: generated SUV is previous concept only. New accurate licensed 3D demonstrator, locally rendered matching posters; two generated macro/editorial concept photographs. No actual project photos supplied.
- Visitor belief: Auto Style can help them discuss a deliberate, personalized vehicle upgrade. No invented service guarantees.
- Main action: Plan my upgrade.

## Feeling curve (written before score)
Recognition: a sculptural car overlaps Auto Style lettering in a restrained studio.
Curiosity: a short editorial statement introduces changing the way a drive feels.
Agency / engineered peak: camera moves around the car; material choices change its finish; a live design brief records the choice.
Intimacy: close-up surfaces and cabin photography reveal the type of details to discuss.
Resolve: a compact enquiry includes selections and real contact paths.

## Grammar
Custom: interactive automotive lookbook. Jump navigation by destination. Poster-like dimensional opening; short natural-flow editorial bridge; one pinned configurator; staggered material spreads; useful enquiry plate. Bans: repeated cards, repeated section counters, continuously fixed page background, fullscreen loading gate, fake reviews, form-success claims.
The eight stock grammars were considered: filmic one-shot prevents useful jumps; chaptered editorial forbids dimensional opening; live surface excludes desired brand narrative; continuous world adds travel without geographic meaning; typographic poster excludes car; gallery schema cannot support the conversion sequence; split stage implies a false before/after; rhythmic cutlist prohibits deliberate inspection time. This custom structure has explicit navigation, sequence, ending and bans.

## Layer contract
Far: pale studio backdrop and stage guide lines, low parallax. Mid: giant real HTML wordmark, recedes on scroll. Subject: licensed physical 3D car with lighting/material response and camera motion. Foreground: corner service annotations and inspect controls, stable and legible. Ground: real rendered contact shadow anchored to tyres. No duplicated photograph under the model. Loading/WebGL fallback uses posters from identical final scene and camera.
Opening: entire car legible below headline, overlapping lower large wordmark. Midpoint: camera opens to side while display type moves away. Exit: grounded car leaves with its own section; next editorial block arrives naturally.
Mobile: dedicated portrait camera framing, shorter travel, static surrounding copy, controls below model; reduced motion removes pins and all scroll transforms.

## Score
| Beat | Device | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Opening | parallax / independent planes | spatial depth, real model |
| Bridge | kinetic reveal in natural flow | quiet comprehension |
| Showroom peak | pin / camera orbit / finish selection | agency, tangible change |
| Upgrade spreads | pan + clip reveal | varied crops and material detail |
| Enquiry | flow + local interaction | clear useful conclusion |
No video. No authored dead scroll. One intentional resolved state on reduced motion only.

## Fingerprint
Registry empty at start. Baseline predecessor differs in hero, nav treatment, sequence, close and signature (5/6). New grammar, destination pill nav, dimensional car poster, short-long-spread-form sequence, enquiry spec card, finish-to-brief signature.
