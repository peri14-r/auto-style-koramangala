# Auto Style Showroom verification

## Execution
Authored brief from the user's explicit redesign request; no new interview. Custom interactive automotive lookbook grammar. Four device families: independent parallax, kinetic line entrance, pinned camera/material scene, clipped and laterally panning photographic spreads. The enquiry is a natural-flow conclusion.

## Research
Pinterest reference was visually inspected through a headless browser: an automotive technician hero, actual-work-style photo crops, high contrast headings and photo-led service presentation. Dribbble's written designer rationale was readable; its shot image did not load in the browser, so no claim of full visual inspection. Jimbo Wash live opening and primary-source case study inspected. Principles adapted: photographic specificity and carrying visitor choices into WhatsApp.

- First harness falsely flagged the bespoke WebGL pin as dead scroll because it did not observe canvas state. Added a signature of the actual rendered camera and material; screenshots confirmed visible camera changes. Subsequent harness found no dead scroll.
- Tested desktop, 390px phone, 320px compact phone, reduced motion and blocked WebGL.
- Finish selection visibly changes the car body. Camera front/side/rear controls work. Actual red side-view screenshot retained.
- Invalid vehicle blocks draft creation. A valid vehicle, service, finish and note all appear in the WhatsApp draft. window.open was intercepted, so no message was sent.
- Keyboard check performed using actual Tab input confirmed a visible outline. A previous programmatic-focus-only check was a test-design failure, not evidence of missing keyboard focus.
- Four scene posters load under WebGL failure; enquiry remains usable.
- A favicon 404 in early runs was fixed by explicit icon metadata.

## Feeling / visual review
Recognition -> curiosity -> agency -> material detail -> resolve. The 3D studio is the largest travel span and largest spatial change. Details now include photographic surfaces rather than repeated text cards. Closing red field resolves into a functional brief form. Total desktop length approximately 7.4 viewport heights rather than a padded cinematic quota. Mobile has its own camera distance and layout, and reduced motion removes pin travel.

## Limits
